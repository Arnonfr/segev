Segev is an artist based in Israel. He works with recycled metal - oil barrels, tin cans, horseshoes and found scrap - alongside wood and paint, building sculptures, masks, wall pieces and furniture.

Fish, masks and faces recur throughout the work: forms cut, welded and riveted from discarded material, often left with the colors and wear of their previous lives.
